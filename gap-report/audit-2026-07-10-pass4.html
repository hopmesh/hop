Completeness critique - Pass 4

The grade table hides a live CI break behind a working-tree fix

The single most important thing this audit is missing: two of the six green ground-truth checks are green only because of uncommitted working-tree edits, and HEAD is red. This is not a "future liability," it is the state of main right now.

1. Apple CI is broken at HEAD. The apple job runs swift test in sdk/wrappers/Hop (ci.yml:216). At HEAD, Hop.swift:143 passes HopLinkRole_Dialer.rawValue (a UInt32) to hop_link_up, whose generated signature takes the enum HopLinkRole type. That is a hard compile error, so swift test cannot even build. The ground-truth "apple-build passed" is true only because the tester fixed it in the working tree (git diff shows Hop.swift modified, uncommitted). On a clean checkout of main, the apple job fails. This should cap both "Apple bearers" and "Apple driver + demo app" until the fix lands, and it means the C-ABI header/Swift binding drifted without anything catching it (the contract-purity job checks the header, not that the Swift consumer compiles against it).

2. Rust CI is flaky at HEAD. The env-var race in hop-relayd (public_stream_is_off_by_default vs public_log_stream_flag_reads_the_env both mutating the process-global HOP_PUBLIC_LOG_STREAM) exists at HEAD. The PUBLIC_LOG_ENV_LOCK mutex that serializes them is working-tree-only and uncommitted. cargo test --workspace (ci.yml:72) can nondeterministically fail on main. The "rust-test-clippy passed" result is contingent on that uncommitted fix.

Neither of these is reflected anywhere in the grade table. "Protocol core: A / A" and the two Apple "B / B+" rows are all resting on edits that are not in the repo. Highest-value gap: get those two fixes committed and pushed before anything else, or the audit's own green baseline is fiction.

LICENSE.md is 0 bytes at HEAD. It was restored in aa4dadb (#35) and re-emptied in the very next commit 86629a8 (#36, "Pass 3 close").
