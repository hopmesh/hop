How it works

Store, carry, forward.

A message doesn't need a live path the instant you send it. Hop holds it, moves it as devices meet, and delivers when a route appears.

→ SEAL

Sealed & addressed

Encrypted to a key. Every relay carries it blind, confidential even across untrusted infrastructure.

→ HOP

Carried by any bearer

BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi, the internet. The bearer changes; the datagram above it doesn't.

→ HOLD

Held, never dropped

No path? A relay keeps it; the backbone holds for offline recipients. Eventual, but guaranteed.

The narrow waist

One layer. Every offline app.

Most offline products rebuild the same transport and weld it to a single app. Hop is that layer, done once, a sealed, store-and-forward datagram. Many bearers below, many apps above.
