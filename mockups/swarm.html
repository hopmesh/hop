A swarm that finds a way.

A city at night, seen from above. Every dot is a device: people on the sidewalks, cars keeping their lane. Watch messages travel hop by hop: BLE down the street canyon, Wi-Fi through the café's router, and up over the internet backbone when a zone has an uplink. Range breathes with the environment (indoors, foliage, walls), and a fresh link needs a moment to secure before it can carry. When no path exists, the message rides in someone's pocket until one opens. Click a device, then another, to send your own. And pick a scenario: the oversubscribed stadium, the basement speakeasy, the disaster, the festival valley, the town with one satellite uplink, the neighborhood where nobody has a data plan. Same physics, same mesh: the infrastructure changes, the messages still arrive.
