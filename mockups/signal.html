How it works

Store, carry, forward.

A message doesn't need a path to exist the moment you send it. Hop holds it, moves it as devices meet, and delivers it when a route appears — minutes or hops later.

1

Seal

Encrypted end to end and addressed to a key — opaque to every relay it crosses.

2

Hop

Carried device to device over whatever bearer is near: Bluetooth, LoRa, Wi-Fi, the internet.

3

Arrive

Held at a relay if the recipient is offline. Delivered the moment they're reachable. Never dropped.
