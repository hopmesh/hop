How it works

Store, carry, forward.

A message doesn't need a path the moment you send it. Hop holds it, moves it as devices meet, and delivers when a route appears, minutes or hops later.

01 · SEAL

Sealed & addressed

Encrypted end to end to a key. Opaque to every relay it crosses, confidential even across untrusted infrastructure.

02 · HOP

Carried by any bearer

BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi, the internet. The bearer changes; the datagram above it doesn't.

03 · ARRIVE

Held, never dropped

No onward path? A relay keeps it. The backbone holds for offline recipients. Eventual, but guaranteed.

The narrow waist

One layer. Every offline app.

Most offline products rebuild the same transport and weld it to a single app. Hop is that layer, done once, a sealed, store-and-forward datagram. Many bearers below, many apps above.
